    Discovery limitations

    Adoptable devices include LAN / Wi-Fi hosts answering PJLink (projectors), Shure control or RTSP (cameras), and Bluetooth headsets and speakers.

    Wireless equipment using UHF/RF, including microphones and in-ear monitors, is not detected yet. This requires an additional RF receiver.

    Manual device registration

    Analogue devices cannot be verified automatically, so they show as Unknown in checks.
